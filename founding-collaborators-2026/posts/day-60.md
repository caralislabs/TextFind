**DAY 60 | WHO WE ARE AND WHAT WE DO**

What if your contributions could continue creating value long after you make them?

For the past several years, I've been developing the foundations of what we're now building at CaralisLabs: **the Execution Economy.**

An environment where people and organizations can contribute ideas, technology, infrastructure, expertise, connections, customers, and much more.

Where independently created capabilities can work together, and where the value generated through their execution could benefit those who helped make it possible.

We've spent years developing and refining the underlying technology. Now we're preparing to welcome our first collaborators.

Over the next **60 days**, we'll be identifying individuals and organizations interested in helping shape this emerging ecosystem.

We're not selling a product.

We're inviting you to explore an opportunity.

**The value of a contribution doesn't have to end when the contribution is made.**

Interested? Send me a private message on LinkedIn. I'd love to hear what you could contribute—or what you'd like to explore.

**60 days remaining.**

**Together, our contributions could become something none of us could easily achieve alone.**

## Introducing CARALIS OS

**CARALIS OS is an execution platform developed by CaralisLabs to enable people and organizations to create, compose, govern, and execute reusable software capabilities.**

Its architecture separates the creation of solutions from the authority to execute them.

AI can assist in translating requirements into executable solutions, while the platform provides independent mechanisms for validation, authorization, execution, and evidence.

CARALIS OS builds upon years of research and development originating with the TextFind platform. Its foundational execution and governance mechanisms have already been implemented and validated, while additional capabilities and user experiences remain under development.

**CARALIS OS provides the technological foundation for the Execution Economy envisioned by CaralisLabs.**

### How the pieces fit together

- **CaralisLabs** is the organization developing the technology and ecosystem.
- **CARALIS OS** is the execution platform under development.
- **The Execution Economy** is the broader vision for enabling contributors to participate in the value their contributions help generate.

CARALIS OS is under active development; this announcement is an introduction and invitation, not a claim of general commercial availability.

## DAY 55 | AI CAN CREATE SOLUTIONS. BUT WHO CONTROLS EXECUTION?

AI is changing how software is created.

We're moving from writing code to describing what we want to accomplish.

But there's a fundamental question:

**Who controls what AI is allowed to execute?**

At CaralisLabs, we're building **CARALIS OS — an execution platform where AI can participate in creating solutions without becoming the ultimate authority over their execution.**

Our foundations are already in place.

We've developed mechanisms for composing reusable capabilities into executable pipelines, managing execution, enforcing authorization, and maintaining execution evidence.

We're now working toward an experience where people can describe what they want to accomplish, and AI can help turn those requirements into governed, executable solutions.

But our vision goes beyond AI.

We believe people and organizations should be able to contribute their ideas, expertise, technology, infrastructure, and connections—and participate in the value those contributions help create.

That's the **Execution Economy** we're developing.

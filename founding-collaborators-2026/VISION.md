# Vision — Participation in the Execution Economy

## Why this initiative exists

CaralisLabs has evolved from developing TextFind as a specialized product toward building a more general foundation for governed execution, capable of supporting TextFind and other applications. The Founding Collaborators initiative invites independent participants to help explore the next stage: an ecosystem in which complementary contributions enable useful execution and potentially share in the value created.

The technological foundation includes work on the Pipeline Execution Runtime (PER), Processing Elements (PEs), pipelines, authorization, Platform Runtimes, Deployment Units, and operational mechanisms. This is ongoing engineering work, **not a claim that a complete commercial ecosystem or economic settlement system is already operational**.

## What participation can mean

People and organizations may contribute ideas, customer introductions, relationships, domain expertise, PEs, pipelines, applications, compute infrastructure, Deployment Unit operations, documentation, testing, training, onboarding support, or contributions we have not yet anticipated.

Participation need not involve programming, and participants may have multiple roles. We aim to make experimentation and collaboration practical through tools, documented interfaces, and suitable onboarding environments as they become available.

## Executional Value

**Executional Value is the value a contribution continues to generate beyond its initial creation, whenever it enables or participates in useful execution.**

A contribution may be reused directly during execution, support the infrastructure that makes execution possible, or indirectly enable execution through a relationship, idea, service, or customer opportunity. These forms of contribution are not necessarily measured in the same way.

**The value of a contribution doesn't have to end when the contribution is made.**

Executional Value is an evolving concept, not a guaranteed payment, financial entitlement, or presently deployed accounting mechanism. Economic attribution and compensation, if any, depend on measurable activity and explicit agreements. Not every execution generates revenue, and not every contribution directly participates in a PER execution.

## From participation to collaboration

The initial 60-day campaign is an invitation to begin conversations and identify a small first cohort. Selected collaborators can help test the contributor experience, identify practical use cases, and shape future participation models. Future commercial mechanisms must be designed, tested, and documented rather than assumed.

## Research provenance, methodology, and IP

This initiative documents a CaralisLabs approach to ecosystem development associated with its existing research on governed execution, execution economics, value attribution, and the Execution Economy. The approach combines a broad invitation to contribute, recognition of direct and indirect contributions, a 60-day public engagement process, and the possibility of ongoing value from useful execution.

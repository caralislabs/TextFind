# CaralisLabs Founding Collaborators — 2026

**An invitation to help shape the Execution Economy.**

CaralisLabs is selecting an initial group of collaborators through a **60-day public invitation and selection process**. We welcome expressions of interest from individuals and organizations of all sizes and backgrounds. Technical development is only one of many possible contributions.

We are developing the infrastructure for governed, composable execution. Our aim is an ecosystem in which people and organizations can contribute capabilities, ideas, infrastructure, expertise, connections, customer opportunities, operations, and more—and potentially benefit from the value those contributions enable over time.

> **The value of a contribution doesn't have to end when the contribution is made.**
>
> **Together, our contributions could become something none of us could easily achieve alone.**

## Participate

Interested in contributing or exploring the possibilities? **Send CaralisLabs a direct message on LinkedIn**, briefly introducing yourself or your organization, what you might contribute, and what you hope to explore.

This is an **early collaborator selection initiative**, not a product launch, an employment offer, or an offer of investment or guaranteed earnings. The first cohort will be small; expressing interest does not guarantee selection. Participation terms will be discussed before substantive work begins.

# Contributor Participation — Initial Principles

## Everyone can have something to contribute

CaralisLabs welcomes expressions of interest from **individuals and organizations**, without restricting participation to software developers or a particular company size. Contributions may include:

- **Ideas and domain knowledge:** potential PEs, pipelines, applications, and business use cases.
- **Relationships and opportunities:** customer introductions, partner connections, and market access.
- **Technology:** PEs, pipelines, integrations, applications, tests, and developer tooling.
- **Infrastructure:** compute capacity, adopted resources, and other operational resources.
- **Operations:** managing Deployment Units, onboarding, customer support, and trials.
- **Enablement:** education, documentation, design, research, feedback, and other expertise.

These are examples, not eligibility categories or an exhaustive list. Selection should be based on fit, feasibility, mutual interest, and applicable legal, security, and operational requirements—not on protected personal characteristics.

## What collaborators may gain

Potential benefits include access to an emerging contributor community, technical learning, experimentation, early feedback, visibility for their work, and opportunities to build independent offerings. Future revenue participation may be possible **only where specific commercial arrangements are agreed**.

No earnings, recurring payments, equity, exclusive status, future customer volume, or selection are promised by this public invitation.

## Ownership and independence

CaralisLabs retains rights to its pre-existing technology, research, publications, and other protected material, subject to existing licenses and agreements. Contributors should retain ownership of their **independently developed contributions**, unless an explicit agreement provides otherwise.

Jointly developed work, use of pre-existing IP, contributions to CaralisLabs repositories, licensing, confidentiality, and commercialization will need written terms appropriate to the activity. Public ideas, messages, introductions, and participation discussions alone do not create a transfer of IP, compensation entitlement, employment relationship, agency, or legal partnership.

## Governance and resource boundaries

Governed execution is distinct from control over external resources. An adopted or externally operated resource does not automatically become CaralisLabs-managed, and an unmanaged PE does not automatically gain access to CaralisLabs-managed resources. Trials and sponsorship arrangements must respect security, consent, resource limits, and applicable agreements.

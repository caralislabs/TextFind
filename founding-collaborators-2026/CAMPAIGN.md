# Founding Collaborators — 60-Day Campaign

## Objective

Invite expressions of interest, engage potential collaborators, and identify a small first cohort for a contributor-readiness phase. This is **not a customer sales launch**.

## Channels and cadence

- Primary channels: LinkedIn and Facebook.
- Planned cadence: one public post per day, labeled **Day 60** through **Day 1**.
- Call to action: contact CaralisLabs via **LinkedIn direct message**.
- Each post explores a different contribution, opportunity, or aspect of the Execution Economy.
- Preserve the final published text and publication references here **after each day's post goes live**. Draft future posts separately until publication.

## Themes

| Campaign period | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Days 60–46 | Invitation, identity, and different kinds of contributions |
| Days 45–31 | Collaboration opportunities, tools, and participation paths |
| Days 30–16 | Governed execution, reuse, Executional Value, and attribution |
| Days 15–1 | Early cohort, readiness, and final expressions of interest |

The daily themes are planning guidance, not commitments to features or benefits.

## Selection and public recognition

The initial cohort will be intentionally small. Participation is subject to mutual fit and appropriate onboarding terms. Publish collaborator names, organizations, contributions, and stories **only with their explicit consent**. Do not promise earnings or disclose confidential business information.
